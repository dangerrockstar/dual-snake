Privacy Policy for Dual Snake

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains how DangerRockstar ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use Dual Snake, a mobile game for Android and iOS.

Information the game stores

Dual Snake does not require an account and does not collect or send personal information to us. It does not include advertising or analytics services. The game saves your best score, sound on/off choice, and game speed preference in local app storage on your device so they are available the next time you play. These values are not sent to us.

The game plays its built-in sounds but does not access the microphone. It does not request your location, contacts, photos, or camera.

How information is used and shared

The locally stored game values are used only to remember your score and preferences. We do not sell, share, or disclose information collected through the game because the game does not transmit user information to us or to advertising or analytics providers.

Your device operating system manages the app's local storage and sound playback. If you download or update the game, the app store and platform providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Those platform practices are separate from information collected by Dual Snake.

This policy page is intended to be hosted by GitHub Pages. When you open the page, GitHub may process standard web-request information, such as your IP address, browser or device information, and the page requested, under GitHub's own privacy policy. This is web hosting information, not information collected by the game.

Retention and deletion

Your score and preferences remain in local app storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. Device or operating-system backup settings may affect whether app data is backed up or restored. Dual Snake has no user accounts or server-held game data to delete.

Children's privacy

The game does not ask any player to provide personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us using the address below.

Changes to this policy

We will update this Privacy Policy whenever our game or data practices change. We will post the revised policy on this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Please review this page periodically for updates.
